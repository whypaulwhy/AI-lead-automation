Privacy policy

Last updated October 6, 2026

Cedar & Slate Roofing is a fictional company. This site is a working demo, built by Prit, of how a roofing company can answer website requests automatically. The request form is real, though. If you send it, your details are handled as described here.

What we collect

When you send the request form, we receive:

  • your name, email address and ZIP code
  • your phone number, if you give one
  • the service you picked and your message
  • that you checked the box allowing us to contact you
  • the time the request arrived and the page it came from

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request by email, usually within a few minutes.
  • To judge how urgent the request is and whether you're in our service area.
  • To keep a record of requests so the office can follow up.

We don't sell your details, use them for advertising, or add you to a mailing list.

Services that handle your details

  • n8n receives the form and runs the steps below. In this demo it runs on our own computer.
  • Anthropic's Claude reads your message to sort the request and write the first line of the reply.
  • Google: Gmail sends the reply, and Google Sheets holds the request log.
  • Slack shows the office an alert with your name, ZIP code, phone number and a short summary.
  • Cal.com handles the booking, if you pick an inspection time through the link in our reply.
  • Google Fonts supplies the lettering on this site. Your browser fetches the font files from Google, so Google sees your IP address.

Each of these services handles data under its own privacy terms.

How long we keep it

We keep requests while this demo is running and delete them when it's reset. You can ask us to delete yours sooner.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details you sent. Reply to any email we sent you and say what you'd like us to do.

Children

This site is meant for adults. Please don't send the form if you're under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
